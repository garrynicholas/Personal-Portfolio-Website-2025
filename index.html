+5 Years in Music Production

From watching launchpad videos to producing my own beats - a chronological story of my love for EDM and sound design.

2016

Fell in Love with the Launchpad

At age 11, I became fascinated by Launchpad performances on YouTube. Watching EDM music being played live inspired me to explore music and freestyle performance.

2017

Super Pads Era

At age 12, I discovered Super Pads on the Play Store and started experimenting with digital Launchpad performances on my phone.

2018 - 2020

First Real DAW

My curiosity about how music was made kept growing. I started researching how artists produced their music, eventually discovering Audio Evolution Mobile and later moving to FL Studio Mobile while learning through YouTube.

2020 - 2022

Going Desktop: FL Studio 20

As my music production skills grew, I became increasingly unsatisfied with mobile production. I eventually switched to FL Studio 20 on desktop, giving me more tools and flexibility to create music.

2022 - Now

Producing & Sharing at 18

At 18, I started taking music production more seriously. YouTube tutorials and Spotify helped me improve my production skills, keep up with trends, and develop my own sound.
